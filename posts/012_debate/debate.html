Intro

During class on Wednesday, we discussed debating with AI. Thus, I started to think about where I debate with other people like friends in my life. I realized that my friends and I often debate where to eat or do have ideas on where to eat. Either none of us have a strong preference for anywhere or we have reasons for not going to a place like “It’s so far”, “I’m not in the mood for that”, etc. I want to try debating with ChatGPT-5 a little about food and hopefully learn some new food recommendations in Philly for me to try next!

Me: You are one of my friends and we have plans to get dinner on Saturday night in Philadelphia, but we do not know where to go. Talk like my friend and debate with me until we find a restaurant to go to.

ChatGPT-5: Okay, so Saturday night in Philly — big question: do we want vibes or do we want food? Like, are we going somewhere because the food is actually amazing, or because it’s fun to hang out there?

I’m leaning toward something in Center City or Fishtown since it’ll feel more like a night out. Maybe Mexican at El Vez? It’s lively, good drinks, pretty classic Philly spot.

But if you’re more in the mood for like a cozy, actually-great-meal kind of night, we could do Zahav (hard to get a reservation, though) or Suraya (super pretty, Mediterranean vibes).

Or… curveball: we could just do something chill like Dim Sum Garden in Chinatown and then go grab boba or dessert after.

What’s the move — do you want trendy or more lowkey?

Me: Hmm probably more lowkey. Do you know any new places that are supposed to be really good?

ChatGPT-5: Okay, cool — I poked around, and a few new(ish) spots caught my eye. I think some of them might hit that lowkey-but-interesting balance we want. Let’s run through a few and you tell me which sound good (or which direction you prefer), and we’ll lock one in.

    • They do scratch-made dishes: katsu curry, wagyu roast beef, “pizza toast,” etc. (Eater Philly)
    • The vibe is supposed to be fun but not overwhelmingly flashy. Could be a great “new but chill” pick.

  2. Borromini (Walnut Street, near Rittenhouse)

    • Italian-forward — pastas, seafood, nice cocktails. If you want something with a more “dinner out” energy, this is strong. (Eater Philly)

  3. El Chingón Fishtown

So between those: Dancerobot and El Chingón are standing out for me (for different reasons). Dancerobot has that “new, cool, unfamiliar” energy, and El Chingón is more laid-back but still flavorful.

Thoughts

I think that my conversation/debate with ChatGPT-5 went pretty well. I confirmed on Resy that Booker’s the final suggestion did indeed have reservations available for this Saturday. ChatGPT’s suggestions at first were definitely new trendy spots in Philly that I had heard of, but it seemed to have missed the realistic detail that they would not be available for this Saturday since they are so popular. Thus, ChatGPT excels at finding new places, but misses some of the more realistic logistics. Overall, I found it easy and fun to chat to about finding a place to eat and I would use it again in the future.
