Intro

It’s October 26th, and if you’re like me, you’re scrambling for a last-minute Halloween costume. I decided to try and ask an LLM to be my costume brainstorming partner, specifically ChatGPT-5. I want my costumes to be achievable, not expensive (hopefully with items I already own), and cute! Let’s see what I’ll be this Halloween!

PROMPT: Help me generate some Halloween costume ideas. I am a girl in college with dark hair and I do not want to buy many new pieces so try to think of costumes that I can make with clothes in my closet already.

Perfect — college Halloween costumes are all about creativity over cost. 🎃 Since you already have dark hair and want to use what’s in your closet, here are 10 costume ideas that are easy to pull together, still cute, and campus-appropriate:

🎬 Pop Culture & Movie-Inspired

  1. Lorelai Gilmore (Gilmore Girls)

    • Pieces: Jeans, a cute top or Henley, a jacket or flannel, coffee cup in hand.
    • Add-ons: Quick wit and caffeine energy.
    • Bonus if your friend goes as Rory.

  2. Wednesday Addams

    • Pieces: Black dress or black skirt + white collared shirt, tights, black shoes.
    • Add-ons: Braided pigtails and a deadpan stare.
    • Dark hair makes this one effortless.

  3. Mia Wallace (Pulp Fiction)

    • Pieces: White button-down, black pants, red lipstick.
    • Add-ons: Black eyeliner, maybe a fake cigarette or “dancing” moment.

  4. Rory Gilmore (student edition)

    • Pieces: Sweater, pleated skirt, collared shirt underneath.
    • Add-ons: A book or backpack—bonus points for a Chilton or Yale reference.

🖤 Classic but Elevated

  1. Vampire

    • Pieces: Black top + black skirt or pants, maybe something lacy or dramatic.
    • Add-ons: Red lipstick, eyeliner wings, dab of fake blood if you have it.

  2. Cat (but make it fashion)

    • Pieces: All black, maybe a bodysuit or black jeans + crop top.
    • Add-ons: Draw whiskers with eyeliner, make ears from paper or headband.

  3. Witch (modern edition)

    • Pieces: All-black outfit + boots, maybe a flowy jacket or skirt.
    • Add-ons: Dark lipstick and jewelry; optional hat or broom.

💫 Fun & Creative

  1. Night Sky

    • Pieces: Navy or black clothes, gold or silver jewelry.
    • Add-ons: Stick on paper stars or glitter; call yourself “the night sky.” ✨

  2. Tourist

    • Pieces: Shorts, sneakers, Hawaiian shirt or tank, baseball cap.
    • Add-ons: Sunglasses, camera, water bottle, fanny pack.

  3. “Hot Girl” Version of Something Random

  • Examples:

    • “Hot Girl Sleep” → Pajamas, slippers, messy bun, eye mask on head.
    • “Hot Girl Study” → Sweatshirt, glasses, laptop, coffee.
    • “Hot Girl Ghost” → White sheet but cut cute holes or crop it.

If you tell me what kinds of clothes you already have (like: mostly neutrals? preppy? sporty? girly?), I can tailor this list so the costumes come directly from your closet. Want me to do that?

I forgot about the Gilmore girls, but that works as well. The classic ones are pretty basic, but always an easy last-minute option. I like the fun and creative ones and think they are quite interesting. I’ve never heard of the “Hot Girl” version of something as a Halloween costume, so I wonder how ChatGPT ended up generating that. Maybe it took something “trendy” and tried applying it to my prompt.

I wonder if we will get better recommendations if I show it a photo of me!

PROMPT: This is what i look like. Can you try to generate more halloween costumes and show what I would look like in them?

Got it — thank you for sharing your photo! Before I generate images, can you confirm a few quick things so I can make the costumes look accurate and in your style:

Do you want the costume previews to stay realistic (like you’re actually wearing the outfit) or be more illustrated/stylized (cartoon, fashion sketch, etc.)?

Would you prefer cute, funny, or low-effort but stylish costumes — or a mix?

Any themes you’d like me to focus on? (e.g. “pop culture,” “clever pun costumes,” “classic spooky,” “girls’ night out energy,” etc.)

Once you confirm that, I can generate realistic examples of how you’d look in a few Halloween costumes that match your vibe and wardrobe.

I was not expecting it to ask me what style I wanted, but I guess they are getting better at trying to do what the user wants which requires more information to begin with.

This was the first image it generated. It definitely used my example image a lot with the same background and a similar top. The person in the image doesn’t really look like me…and there is a broom and a witch hat, which I don’t think many people have just lying around. I wanted to see if it would generate a less basic costume so I asked it, “can you generate more please and try to make it less basic?”

andddd I got a cat. I don’t believe that a cat is less basic in my opinion. Both of these images did not meet my expectations as they were not very creative and used very classic costumes. So, I went back to just prompting with text.

PROMPT: Since you know what I look like now, can you generate more Halloween costume ideas that you think I would look good as? Try to think of ones that are not as common

Love that — you’ve got a really approachable, warm vibe and natural confidence, so you can pull off costumes that are a little clever or subtly striking without being over-the-top. Here are 10 more unique Halloween costume ideas that would fit your look, and that you could probably make from clothes you already own:

✨ 1. Galaxy Girl

  • Pieces: Black or navy top + skirt or jeans, silver or sparkly jewelry.
  • Add-ons: Dot on “stars” with glitter or white eyeliner; maybe a hair clip shaped like a moon.
  • Gives mysterious, pretty, but low-effort celestial energy.

📸 2. “Main Character in an Indie Movie”

  • Pieces: Oversized sweater, vintage-looking jeans, tote bag, headphones.
  • Add-ons: Carry a coffee cup or book; lean into “moody fall film aesthetic.”
  • Cute, meta, and very you—people will get it instantly.

🕯️ 3. Cottagecore Ghost

  • Pieces: Long white dress or flowy top + skirt.
  • Add-ons: Sheer white scarf over your head, or even a lacy bedsheet.
  • Mix spooky and soft—a poetic twist on a classic ghost.

📚 4. Jane Austen Heroine

  • Pieces: Light blouse, midi skirt, maybe a cardigan or wrap.
  • Add-ons: Updo hair, a little blush, small book as prop.
  • Looks refined and romantic without needing a costume store.

🌹 5. “Love Potion”

  • Pieces: Red or pink outfit; something flowy or satiny if you have it.
  • Add-ons: Carry a small bottle labeled “Love Potion No. 9.”
  • Playful and flirty, especially for a party setting.

🎧 6. Spotify Wrapped

  • Pieces: Bright, color-blocked clothes.
  • Add-ons: Tape or print song titles on your shirt, or use colored sticky notes.
  • Creative, easy, and a conversation starter.

🧃 7. Juice Box

  • Pieces: Brightly colored outfit (like orange, pink, or purple).
  • Add-ons: Cardboard straw taped to a headband.
  • Silly and unexpected, but still cute.

🎀 8. Retro Flight Attendant

  • Pieces: Button-up blouse + pencil skirt or trousers, scarf tied at neck.
  • Add-ons: Small vintage bag, red lipstick.
  • Chic and powerful with a throwback twist.

🌿 9. Botanical Fairy

  • Pieces: Green, cream, or earthy outfit; add fake leaves or flowers.
  • Add-ons: Subtle shimmer makeup, maybe a crown of twigs or petals.
  • Pretty and otherworldly without being cliché.

📖 10. Greek Muse

  • Pieces: White dress or sheet toga-style, gold accessories.
  • Add-ons: Carry a notebook or lyre-like prop.
  • Elegant, classic, and fits perfectly with your calm, creative look.

Would you like me to generate realistic visuals of three of these (like I did before)? If so, I can pick the ones that’ll look best based on your vibe — or you can choose your top three.

These are definitely unique ideas… I certainly have not heard of a lot of them and am wondering how ChatGPT-5 generated these. I’ve never heard of someone being Spotify Wrapped…

Thoughts

Overall, I think that ChatGPT-5 is certainly inventive and creative, but a lot of the costumes it generated were too strange. For example, I don’t think that I woule be recognizable as a Jane Austen Heroine. However, I enjoyed brainstorming with it and seeing what it would come up with!
