Carmack and his coworkers John Romero and Tom Hall, along with Jay Wilbur and Lane Roathe, developed a demo of a PC version of Super Mario Bros. 3, but failed to convince Nintendo to invest in a PC port of their game. Game Description

The story begins with Commander Keen falling asleep while playing his favorite Nintendo game Super Mario Bros. 3 , which he purchased at a garage sale. He wakes up, opens the cupboard under his bed, and finds himself thrust into a strange alien world that looks like Mars but is actually Earth. The only other people in this new planet are aliens living on two continents, one of which has an area roughly similar to the Grand Canyon.

Keen quickly discovers that he must rescue three stolen parts from the cities of each continent before the aliens can destroy all life on Earth with their Vorticon weapon. Keen finds the first part hidden within a city in Africa and begins his journey across the continents, encountering various enemies along the way while fighting to find the missing pieces.

The game features an original storyline that explores some of the themes and motifs found in later Commander Keen games, including humor, adventure, exploration, and puzzle-solving. The story also features many of the memorable characters from subsequent episodes of the series such as Dr. Zin and the Grand Intellect.

Gameplay

The gameplay is similar to that of its predecessor Doom 3, though with a more linear level design. Keen runs across various platforms while jumping from one to another, using his pistol (the only weapon he has) to shoot enemies or obstacles that may block his path. He can also climb onto ledges by pressing the down arrow and pull back on jump buttons to do so.

Keen’s ship is equipped with two thrusters in each corner that he uses to move across the screen while running around platforms; these are controlled via a toggle switch located next to Keen’s pistol, which must be held at all times during use. To shoot enemies or obstacles, Keen simply presses the button on his gun (a small red dot appears on it when he is close enough), and when an enemy comes into range, one of two shots will hit them; if they are too far away for a shot to land, another option can be used to fire a missile which explodes upon impact. The game also includes some puzzles that require Keen to solve in order to advance.

The gameplay style is reminiscent of earlier platform games such as Doom 3 and Super Mario Bros. , though with an emphasis on combat. The level design also features many unique environments, including ice worlds and underwater levels which are usually visited only once in the series.

Review

Reception

The Computer Gaming World magazine awarded the trilogy four stars out of five, calling it “a truly awesome package.” The reviewer felt that this was a significant improvement over Super Mario Bros. 3, due to its more open gameplay and smoother graphics. He described Keen’s character as “witty”, while praising his ability to jump over dangerous obstacles.

The Computer Game Review magazine gave the trilogy five out of five stars, calling it “one of the most exciting video games of all time” and a game that should be in every computer gamer’s library. The reviewer praised Keen for being able to “jump, shoot, punch and kick enemies with ease.” He also commended the fact that the game was not as frustrating as other video games at the time because it had only three main enemies and they were easy to defeat.
